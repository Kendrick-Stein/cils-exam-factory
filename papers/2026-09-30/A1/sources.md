## Procedure and copyright gate

Pool-first selection used exact level, genre and band for every slot. Salaborsa T1 was a pool match; its explicit NC-SA license was subsequently verified. Other pool candidates lacked documented permission for adaptations and publication and were not accepted solely on public availability. Wikimedia projects are an off-list exception for verifiable open licensing and authentic real-world genres. All accepted URLs were checked against historical blacklist, including decoded equivalence, and against other current level source lists.

Do not print source lines in the student text. Publish accessible attribution/license/change notices with the answers or alongside the PDFs. A private manifest alone is insufficient. Distinguish CC BY-SA excerpts from CC BY-NC-SA excerpts; do not impose NC on BY-SA material. No images, external linked documents, or separately owned quotations copied. Salaborsa NC permission is used only for this coordinator-confirmed free, noncommercial practice batch.

Microtext totals refer to item bodies, with source-based continuations drawn from retained original tails. S1 and S3 cloze originals have room for deleted gap words. Titles may be simple editorial labels, but new factual prose is forbidden.
